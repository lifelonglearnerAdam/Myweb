# Poetry interludes · 诗句出处

所有诗句都是下列作者的作品短摘，不包含生成诗、仿作、原创续写或虚构署名。中英文页面均保留英文原文，不加中文翻译或解释。

1. **Robert Frost — The Road Not Taken**
   - “Two roads diverged in a wood, and I— / I took the one less traveled by,”
   - Source: https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/44272/the-road-not-taken
   - Only a short excerpt is used, not the complete poem. The page does not present the excerpt as a definitive interpretation of the poem.
2. **Rabindranath Tagore — Stray Birds, 6**
   - “If you shed tears when you miss the sun, you also miss the stars.”
   - Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/6524
   - Verified against the English text. Visual line wrapping is adjusted for the card; wording is unchanged.
3. **William Blake — Auguries of Innocence**
   - “To see a World in a Grain of Sand / And a Heaven in a Wild Flower,”
   - Source: https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/43650/auguries-of-innocence
4. **Emily Dickinson — “Hope” is the thing with feathers**
   - ““Hope” is the thing with feathers — / That perches in the soul —”
   - Source: https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/42889/hope-is-the-thing-with-feathers-314
   - Dash typography is normalized for display, not rewritten.
5. **William Wordsworth — The Tables Turned**
   - “Come forth into the light of things, / Let Nature be your teacher.”
   - Source: https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/45557/the-tables-turned

`/` above marks a line break, not added verse. Poetry cards link the work titles to these sources.
